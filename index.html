About

I'm a marketing student at Fordham University's Gabelli School of Business with a minor in creative writing, graduating May 2027. I like working at the intersection of data and creative work, whether that's analyzing SEO, sales, or customer data to optimize marketing strategies or writing a poem on a Saturday afternoon.

At Platypus Media, I've developed SEO and media monitoring tools using Python, led the brand launch for a new publishing imprint, and created social and advertising content across platforms. I've also interned at Guilford Press and The Center for Fiction.

Outside of work, I write poetry and fiction (I studied at NYU Paris in summer 2024), manage the budget for Fordham's literary magazine, and build side projects like Squeakyturtle, a budget tracking app I made with Claude Code.
